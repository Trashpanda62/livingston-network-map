# Network-map ripple of the five proposed properties

The live map stands at **82 nodes / 85 edges** today.

Each property adds one placard to the online-layer sign plus a `lists` edge to every
Overton County business on its roster. Businesses already on the map gain an edge rather
than a pin. The county filter applies as it does to Middle TN Printers: regional listings
outside Overton County stay off the map while remaining on the site itself.

**Corrected 2026-08-14.** An earlier version of this table counted a business as Overton
County because the SEARCH QUERY named a Livingston town, not because the business was
there. That mistake put a restaurant in Livingston, LOUISIANA on the Overton list, along
with businesses in Pickett, Clay, Campbell and Macon counties. County is now derived from
each business's harvested street address, and records with no address are reported as
unverified rather than counted either way.
